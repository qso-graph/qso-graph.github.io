Products

QSO Graph is several products, not one program. Each stands alone: use the one that applies to you and ignore the rest. They follow the same specification, so moving between them is easy.

Applications

ProductWhat it isVersion
qso-graph-desktopQSO Graph Desktop: a free desktop home for your station, on Windows, macOS and Linux, with your logins in your OS credential store.v0.7.1
qso-graph-trustQSO Graph Trust (QGT): sign and upload logs to ARRL's Logbook of The World, with your key kept in your OS credential store.v0.2.3
